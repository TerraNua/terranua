## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory, grounded elegance of handcrafted natural soaps from Sinaloa. The aesthetic bridges artisanal heritage with contemporary botanical luxury: warm, tactile, and mindful. It deliberately avoids clinical laboratory visuals in favor of earth-grown textures, quiet indulgence, and botanical warmth.

The visual style blends **Tactile Organic Warmth** with **Editorial Editorial Minimalism**. Key principles:
- **Rooted Serenity:** Clean layouts balanced by soft, earthy transitions and organic negative space.
- **Craft & Substance:** Subtle paper-like grain, natural earth tones, and warm, sunlit shadows rather than synthetic digital blurs.
- **Botanical Intimacy:** Elegant serif headers evoking apothecary labels, paired with a welcoming, readable sans-serif structure for frictionless commerce.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in raw natural botanicals, clays, and sun-warmed earth:

- **Primary (`#2F4A1F` - Deep Olive Leaf):** The grounding botanical anchor. Used for primary CTAs, high-priority navigation points, key headlines, and focused states.
- **Secondary (`#B5654A` - Warm Terracotta Clay):** Brings warmth and artisanal tactility. Used for badges, secondary buttons, seasonal highlights, and subtle borders.
- **Tertiary (`#B8892B` - Golden Honey / Miel):** Reflects organic raw honey and calendula extracts. Applied selectively to review stars, botanical certifications, active badges, and delicate accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F5F0E6` - Warm Linen Cream):** Base surface for the entire experience. Softens contrast against the deep olive text to prevent digital eye strain and maintain a handcrafted parchment quality.
- **Accent Wine / Deep Berry (`#7A1F33`):** Reserved for luxury apothecary collections, seasonal product markers, and alert/status micro-accents.
- **Earth Coffee (`#6B4A3A`):** Used for subtle supporting metadata, ingredient profiles, and secondary body accents.
- **Surface Elevation Steps:**
  - Base: `#F5F0E6` (Cream Canvas)
  - Surface Mid: `#EDE6D8` (Parchment Tone for cards and modules)
  - Surface Border: `rgba(107, 74, 58, 0.15)` (Tierra borders)

## Typography

Typography balances traditional editorial grace with tactile approachability:
- **Playfair Display** anchors all expressive statements, product titles, and editorial lead-ins. It delivers the refinement of botanical handbooks and luxury apothecary traditions.
- **Nunito Sans** carries all informational text, checkout processes, and nutritional/ingredient details. Its subtle rounded terminals harmonize with the handcrafted nature of soap bars without appearing child-like.
- **Botanical Detail Accents:** Botanical latin names and batch notes should be rendered in `body-sm` italic with low-contrast earth tone (`#6B4A3A`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive 12-column grid system configured to prioritize breathing space, tactile product photography, and sensory browsing:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer canvas `margin: 3rem` (max-width constrained to `1280px` for optimal reading flow).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, outer `margin: 2rem`.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, outer `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`.

Rhythmic spacing uses multiples of 8px, prioritizing generous vertical padding (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between narrative sections to evoke an unhurried, artisanal studio experience.

## Elevation & Depth

To remain faithful to artisanal materials and avoid synthetic neon gloss, depth relies on **sun-warmed ambient shadows** and **tonal paper layering**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Warm linen neutral `#F5F0E6`. Flat.
- **Layer 1 (Card/Container Surfaces):** Muted earthen parchment `#EDE6D8` with a delicate 1px border tinted in `rgba(107, 74, 58, 0.12)`.
- **Warm Ambient Shadow (Resting):** `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(107, 74, 58, 0.08)`. The shadow hue uses the warm café/tierra tone rather than neutral cold gray.
- **Elevated/Hover Shadow:** `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(47, 74, 31, 0.14)`. Infused with subtle olive tint to simulate a soft natural ground shadow.
- **Floating Modals & Drawers:** `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(47, 74, 31, 0.20)` paired with a soft canvas backdrop wash `rgba(245, 240, 230, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness tier of `2` (0.5rem base radius). This reflects the naturally smoothed, beveled edges of hand-poured, cured glycerin soap blocks:

- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** 0.5rem (`8px`) border radius. Never strictly square or clinical; never full oval pills, retaining structural craft.
- **Product & Story Cards:** 0.75rem to 1rem (`12px` - `16px`) corner softening.
- **Feature Imagery Containers:** Asymmetrical organic framing (e.g., top-left and bottom-right 1.5rem, alternate corners 0.5rem) can be selectively utilized for botanical hero photography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Olive (`#2F4A1F`) background, `#F5F0E6` text, 0.5rem border radius. Hover introduces a warm shift to Terracotta (`#B5654A`) with an ambient lift.
- **Secondary / Craft:** Warm Linen surface (`#EDE6D8`), 1.5px border in Terracotta (`#B5654A`), text in Olive (`#2F4A1F`).
- **Text & Editorial Links:** Playfair Display italic or Nunito 600 weight with an organic terracotta underline situated 4px beneath the baseline.

### Cards (Product & Story)
- Background `#EDE6D8` with a hairline border `rgba(107, 74, 58, 0.15)`.
- Image frame displays natural soap bars and herbal botanicals with soft 0.5rem inner radius.
- Includes ingredient provenance pill (e.g., *"Glicerina vegetal & Caléndula de Culiacán"*).

### Chips & Botanical Tags
- Compact tags with 0.375rem radius.
- Background in translucent Terracotta or Golden Honey (`rgba(184, 137, 43, 0.12)`), text in `#6B4A3A`, letter-spacing `0.04em`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FBF8F3` (slightly lighter than base canvas), 1px solid border `rgba(107, 74, 58, 0.25)`.
- Focused state: 1.5px border in Deep Olive (`#2F4A1F`) accompanied by a subtle warm glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(47, 74, 31, 0.12)`.
- Labels in `label-sm`, uppercase, `#6B4A3A`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular or soft-cornered frames in `#2F4A1F` when selected.
- Unselected state uses natural earthen outline (`#6B4A3A`).

### Specialized Component: Botanical Ingredient Profile
- Two-column or horizontal capsule showcasing the botanical source (e.g., *Miel de Azahar*, *Arcilla Roja*), visual harvest icon, skin benefit tags, and natural scent intensity meter (graded from 1 to 5 leaves).
